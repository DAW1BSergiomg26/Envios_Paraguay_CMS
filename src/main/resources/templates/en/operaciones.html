Operations

How we work. We handle your heavy cargo with the safety and care it deserves.

At MONTEASTUR ENVIOS we coordinate each shipment in a personalised way. From the collection of your merchandise in Pola de Siero to final delivery in Paraguay, our team handles the entire logistics process, including customs management.

We work with vehicles, machinery, heavy cargo and all types of merchandise. Each shipment receives a treatment adapted to its characteristics, with the appropriate documentation and transport to ensure it arrives in perfect condition.

🚚

Pickup in Asturias

We pick up your merchandise in Pola de Siero and surrounding areas. We schedule the appointment at your convenience. You can also drop it off at our collection point.

💼

Documentation & customs

We manage all customs procedures and the necessary documentation so your cargo leaves Asturias and enters Paraguay without issues.

🚗

Specialised transport

We select the best transport option for your type of cargo: vehicles, machinery, heavy cargo or general merchandise. Total flexibility.

📦

Delivery in Paraguay

Final distribution in Asunción and main cities of Paraguay. Personalised tracking until delivery.
